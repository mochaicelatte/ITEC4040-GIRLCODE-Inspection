## 3. Issue Types

Issues may be classified as:

- Missing or incomplete requirement
- Incorrect or conflicting requirement
- Ambiguous requirement
- Duplicate or unnecessary requirement
- Unverifiable requirement
- Quality requirement issue
- Organization or traceability issue
- Diagram or use-case inconsistency

## 4. Severity Guidelines

- **Major:** An issue that significantly affects requirement correctness, completeness, consistency, or verifiability.
- **Minor:** An issue that affects clarity, organization, or documentation without significantly changing intended functionality.

Severity classifications are provisional until confirmed during the inspection meeting.
